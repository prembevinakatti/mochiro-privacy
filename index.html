Mochiro • Official Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

🌿 Summary: Mochiro is an offline-first focus companion. Version 1 is 100% free with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads, no analytics, and no remote data tracking. Your focus history and settings remain entirely on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mochiro ("we", "our", or "the app"), published on Google Play by developer organization BORN2CODE (lead developer: Onkar Bevinakatti, application ID: com.born2code.mochiro). Mochiro is a peaceful focus companion and study timer designed to help you build distraction-free productivity habits alongside your soft-3D Mochi pal.

We believe your daily productivity, study sessions, and focus habits belong exclusively to you. This Privacy Policy outlines our zero-data collection architecture, explains how local on-device storage operates, clarifies how Android system permissions are utilized, and details how Android Auto Backup interacts with your local data.

2. Zero Data Collection & Zero Telemetry

Mochiro is designed with an uncompromising commitment to your privacy:

  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not integrate Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment, or any other user telemetry or tracking service.
  • No Advertising: Mochiro contains zero advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, or AppLovin). We never profile your activity, display banners, or monetize your attention.
  • No User Account or Login: You do not need to register, provide an email address, enter a phone number, or log in with Google, Apple, or social media to use Mochiro.
  • No Location, Contacts, or Microphone Tracking: We never request access to your GPS location, contact directory, microphone, camera, or device call logs.

3. Local On-Device Data Storage & Auto Backup

All focus data, streaks, and customization in Mochiro are processed and stored exclusively on your device:

  • Local SQLite Database: Completed focus sessions, timestamps, session duration, and companion usage are recorded in a private, sandboxed, on-device SQLite database (via expo-sqlite). This database is stored in the application's private internal storage directory and is not shared with any third party.
  • Local Preferences (MMKV): User preferences—such as Do Not Disturb mode (Priority vs. Total Silence), sound and haptic toggles, reduce-motion settings, chosen timer font, active timer color preset, selected Mochi companion (from the 4 free companions), and selected living atmosphere (from the 4 free atmospheres)—are saved locally in high-speed key-value storage.

Android Auto Backup Disclosure

Mochiro has Android Auto Backup enabled (android:allowBackup="true"). If you have enabled Android Backup in your device settings, Android may automatically back up your local app database and user preferences to your personal Google Drive account associated with your device. When you reinstall Mochiro or set up a new Android device with the same Google account, Android may restore this local data. This backup is managed directly by Google Android OS under your own Google account security settings; Mochiro developers never receive or access your backup files.

4. Android Device Permissions & Usage

Mochiro requests only the Android permissions necessary to provide the focus timer and anti-distraction features you explicitly select:

Permission Name Manifest Identifier Purpose in Mochiro
Do Not Disturb Access android.permission.ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY Optional focus feature. When enabled by the user, Mochiro temporarily activates your chosen Do Not Disturb filter during a focus session to mute incoming notification rings and vibrations. Mochiro automatically restores your previous interruption filter as soon as the session ends or is cancelled.
Notification Posting android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13+ solely to alert you when your focus timer has finished, letting you know your session is complete if your display is off or another app is open.
Haptic Feedback android.permission.VIBRATE Drives tactile vibration feedback when pressing timer controls, stepping duration, completing a session, or holding the 5-second Hold-to-Quit emergency exit.
Boot Completed android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED It restores the timer-end alert after a restart.
Wake Lock android.permission.WAKE_LOCK Briefly awakens device CPU to sound the timer completion chime when a session finishes while the screen is asleep.

Screen Pinning (Lock Task Mode)

Mochiro's full-screen focus lock uses Android's official startLockTask() system API. This is not a manifest permission; it is an Android OS feature that temporarily pins the app to your screen to eliminate notification interruptions and impulsive phone checking. You can exit Screen Pinning at any moment by holding down the in-app 5-second Hold-to-Quit button or by using Android's standard system unpin key combination (Back + Overview buttons).

Blocked / Unused Permissions

Mochiro explicitly blocks all non-essential and sensitive permissions using tools:node="remove" in its release manifest:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Blocked. Mochiro v1 is 100% offline-first. External links open directly in your device's browser without requiring internet permission for Mochiro.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Blocked. Mochiro does not run background services.
  • android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Blocked. System audio hardware routing is untouched.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Blocked. Mochiro v1 is completely free with no in-app purchases.
  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO: Blocked. Mochiro synthesizes audio locally and never records microphone audio.
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE & WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Blocked. Mochiro uses private sandboxed storage.

5. In-App Purchases & Payments

Mochiro is currently completely free, with no in-app purchases, paid subscriptions, or payment processing. All 4 Mochi companions, 4 living atmospheres, timer typography, and focus analytics are fully unlocked for every user. This privacy policy will be updated before any paid feature ships.

6. Network Dependencies & Third-Party Code Audit

To ensure complete transparency regarding network activity:

  • In-App Billing (RevenueCat): Disabled. Mochiro v1 runs in free mode (V1_MONETIZATION = 'free'). The billing SDK initialization is bypassed, and billing permissions are removed from the release manifest.
  • Expo Updates: Disabled in the release manifest (expo.modules.updates.ENABLED = false). The app does not download remote JavaScript bundles or over-the-air patches.
  • External Browser (expo-web-browser): Used strictly when you tap an external link (such as opening this Privacy Policy or the developer website). This launches your device's default web browser outside of Mochiro.

7. Children's Privacy

Mochiro is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. Mochiro does not collect, store, or transmit personal information from any user, including children. If you have questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact us.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Mochiro does not store your data on remote servers or in cloud databases:

  • Immediate Local Deletion: You can completely erase all local focus history and preferences at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Mochiro → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling Mochiro permanently removes the local SQLite database and stored preferences from your device.
